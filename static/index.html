Descriptors & similarity

Mass: average residues + water, optionally C-amidated. Charge/pI: Henderson–Hasselbalch. GRAVY and hydrophobic moment use Kyte–Doolittle; the moment and wheel assume an ideal 100° α-helix, not a predicted structure. Extinction assumes reduced cysteines.

Sequence score: 100 × (1 − global edit distance / longer length). Alignment identity includes gap columns in the denominator. Property score: 100 / (1 + scaled RMS descriptor difference). Neither score establishes antimicrobial function.
